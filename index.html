Our story

Two proud clubs. One community.

Coastal Spirit was formed in late 2007 when Rangers, founded in 1910, and New Brighton, founded in 1924, joined forces. Today the club welcomes beginners, social players and elite athletes across men's, women's and junior football.

Read the full history →

  1. 1910 & 1924Rangers and New Brighton are founded, two of Christchurch's oldest football clubs.
  2. 2007The clubs merge to become Coastal Spirit, based at Bexley Park.
  3. 2011The Canterbury earthquakes destroy the Bexley facilities. The club rebuilds at Linfield Park and Cuthberts Green.
  4. 2020Offices and clubrooms move to Linfield Sports.
  5. 2025Maiden Southern League title for the Men's Premier team.
  6. 2027Coastal Spirit joins the new 12-team Dettol National League.
